Website privacy

A little clarity
about your information.

This notice describes the information website and its enquiry and feedback forms. The CareSys application is a separate service.

What the website collects

The contact form collects the details you enter, such as your name, email, organization, phone number, plan interest and message. The feedback form collects your name, perspective, rating, written feedback and publication preference. Do not provide patient details, passwords or confidential employment records.

Why we collect it

Contact details help us review and respond to enquiries. Feedback helps us understand experiences and improve the product. Feedback is not published automatically. Only feedback with publication permission can be selected for publication, using the first name and perspective you agreed to share.

How the local website works

In this local preview, submissions are stored in a private website inbox on the local machine. Email delivery is not configured. The site does not connect to the CareSys application database or reuse your application sign-in. This preview has no advertising or analytics trackers and does not set tracking cookies.

Access and deletion

Only the person operating the website should access the private inbox. Use the contact form to request correction or deletion and provide the reference from your submission, without sending further sensitive details. A confirmed retention period and production contact address must be published before public launch.

Videos and application links

Videos are hosted by this website rather than embedded from an external tracking platform. Links to the application open a separate host with its own authentication. Product videos contain fictional demonstration records.

Before public launch

This is a local development website. Public hosting requires a confirmed organization contact, a retention policy, HTTPS, reviewed consent wording, managed inbox access and any jurisdiction-specific privacy terms.
