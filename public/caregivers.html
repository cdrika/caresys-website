YOUR OWN STARTING POINT

A personal account.
Before an agency relationship.

Whether you are exploring your first role or planning a new step in your career, you can start by registering yourself. Enter an account name, your email address and a password in the application.

Your personal workspace gives you a place to describe your experience, manage preferences, find opportunities and follow your applications. An agency relationship comes later, through the appropriate offer and joining decisions.

  • No agency invitation needed to register
  • Save and complete your profile at your own pace
  • Apply directly to available published vacancies
  • Keep control of recruitment visibility and selected history
FROM YOUR PROFILE TO YOUR NEXT STEP

Here is how to get started.

01

Register your personal account

Open the application registration page. Create an account using your own account name, email and password. You do not need to belong to an agency first.

02

Add your information

Complete your profile with a professional summary, skills, qualifications, languages, work type and preferred locations. Add career experience and keep information up to date.

03

Choose what recruiters can discover

A saved profile can stay private. To publish a recruitment summary, complete the required verification and profile details, review the preview and confirm publication when eligible. You can pause discovery.

04

Browse opportunities and apply

Select Find work in your personal workspace. Read open vacancies from verified agencies, check the work area and requirements, then apply to a role that interests you.

05

Follow your applications

See your submitted applications and their current stage in Find work. Respond to invitations and withdraw an application when appropriate. Decisions and response times depend on the agency.

06

Review offers and make your choice

Review the specific offer version before deciding. Share selected career history only when you authorize it. Joining an agency is a separate decision; an application alone does not make you an employee.

USEFUL TO KNOW

A few questions before you begin.

Can I register without an agency inviting me?

Yes. Use Create your personal account to register independently. You can build your profile and browse available opportunities before an agency relationship exists.

Do I need one of the agency subscription plans?

No agency subscription purchase is required for the personal registration and application path described here. The Starter, Operations and Growth prices on this website are agency plans.

Is publishing my profile the same as applying for a job?

No. Publishing an eligible recruitment summary lets authorized recruiters discover it. Applying submits an application to a specific published vacancy. You can use the Find work area to browse and apply.

Are there checks before recruiters can discover my profile?

Yes. The current application requires verification, completed profile details and confirmation of the recruitment preview before discovery. Recruitment discovery while already actively employed through CareSys is currently restricted under the pilot policy; your personal account remains available.

Can I see the progress of an application?

Yes. Your personal Find work area shows your applications and their current stage. An agency controls its recruitment decisions and timing. Applying does not guarantee an interview, offer or placement.

Does registering expose all my career information?

No. Recruitment visibility and selected career-history sharing are separate choices. History access is tied to your approved information and named recipients, with the relevant expiry and withdrawal controls.

Can I do this in the web application today?

The current local web application supports personal registration, profiles and vacancy applications. Native Android and iPhone apps remain planned. In this local preview, the account links open the separate application host.
