FOLLOW YOUR PATH

Start with your role.

For agency teams

Get familiar with your agency workspace.

  1. Sign in and choose the intended agency workspace. Your role determines the modules you can access.
  2. Review recruitment summaries and manage applications with a clear purpose.
  3. Review the specific offer version and joining conditions before activation.
  4. Plan visits, check assignments and review time, leave and care records in the relevant modules.
For individual caregivers

Make your next career step your own.

  1. Create your personal profile and add skills, qualifications and work preferences.
  2. Review your recruitment preview before making it discoverable. Pause visibility when you choose.
  3. Browse opportunities and respond to applications or invitations. Employer response times vary.
  4. Review offers and choose any selected career history you want to share. Joining requires your permission.
Mobile pairing

Try the device authentication flow locally.

  1. Open the development test-device page on the separate API host and generate a real pairing code.
  2. Open the approval page, sign in and enter that code within five minutes.
  3. Check the device name, confirm your password and approve the request.
  4. Return to the test device to verify access. Disconnect when finished. Native apps remain planned.
