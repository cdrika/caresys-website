Module information

The moving parts.
Working together.

Explore the workflows that connect your people, everyday care and agency records. Start with the area that matters most to your team.

01PEOPLE

People & recruitment

Find the right people. Keep the decisions clear.

Caregivers browse published vacancies and apply from their personal workspace. Agencies review applications, discover eligible approved summaries and manage invitations and recruitment stages.

  • Independent caregiver applications
  • Approved profile discovery
  • Recruiter review and invitations
See this workflow ↗
02PEOPLE

Profiles & privacy

A professional identity that stays with you.

Independent caregivers can register their own personal account, add experience, skills, qualifications and work preferences, then choose recruitment visibility and selected career history sharing.

  • Guided personal profiles
  • Discovery pause and preferences
  • Named-recipient history consent
See this workflow ↗
03PEOPLE

Offers & joining

Make every next step an explicit choice.

Review contract versions, record decisions and authorize joining separately before an agency activates the relationship.

  • Version-specific offers
  • Password-confirmed decisions
  • Conditions and effective dates
See this workflow ↗
04OPERATIONS

Scheduling & visits

Give planned work a shared place.

Coordinate client visits and caregiver assignments with checks for overlapping work, leave and required qualifications.

  • Client visit planning
  • Assignment eligibility checks
  • Agency-scoped schedules
See this workflow ↗
05OPERATIONS

Time & attendance

From clock events to reviewed time.

Record online clock events and breaks, review worked time and handle correction requests with a record of decisions.

  • Online clock and break events
  • Worked-time review
  • Version-aware corrections
See this workflow ↗
06OPERATIONS

Leave & requests

Keep availability decisions connected.

Submit and review leave requests, maintain balances and account for approved leave while planning assignments.

  • Leave requests and decisions
  • Balance posting and reversal
  • Scheduling conflict checks
See this workflow ↗
07OPERATIONS

Care notes & incidents

Keep the context close to the work.

Record visit notes and incidents in the agency workspace so the right team can review and follow up.

  • Visit-related notes
  • Incident records
  • Acknowledgements and follow-up
See this workflow ↗
08BUSINESS

Equipment & expenses

Keep track of what moves through your agency.

Register equipment, record custody and returns, and review expenses with evidence for reimbursement reconciliation.

  • Equipment custody history
  • Expense review
  • Reimbursement evidence
See this workflow ↗
09BUSINESS

Billing & payroll preparation

Bring business records into view.

Maintain invoices, receipts and approved worked-time export snapshots. External payroll submission and accounting adapters remain future work.

  • Invoice and receipt records
  • Worked-time export snapshots
  • Review and reporting workflows
See this workflow ↗

Clear about what is available.

These workflows describe the current local application. Native mobile screens, production payment processing, external payroll submission and accounting integrations are not included as completed capabilities. Plan scope, module entitlements and pilot readiness are confirmed with each agency.
