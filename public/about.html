About CareSys

Care is personal.
Coordination should be clearer.

CareSys connects the practical work of a care agency with the professional journey of the people who make care possible.

WHY CARESYS

A shared workspace.
A place for each person.

Recruitment, scheduling, time reviews and business records all carry important decisions. CareSys gives those decisions a connected place, with clear roles and a record of what happened.

At the same time, an individual caregiver keeps a personal professional profile. Joining an agency does not mean giving up control of that identity or opening every part of a career history.

PRINCIPLES IN PRACTICE
01

People keep their identity.

02

03
