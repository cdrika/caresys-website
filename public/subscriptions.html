A FEW USEFUL DETAILS

Before you choose.

What counts as an active worker?

The proposed measure is a unique worker with an active employment or assignment in the agency during the billing period, counted once per agency. Candidate profiles and inactive archived records do not count. Final billing definitions are confirmed in the agreement.

Can I pay or start a subscription here?

Not yet. This website collects enquiries and demo requests. Online checkout is planned. Discuss the scope, module availability, taxes and start date with us before any subscription agreement.

Is payroll submission included?

No. The current application prepares approved worked-time export snapshots. Provider-specific payroll submission and full accounting integrations remain future work.

Can these prices change?

These are provisional pilot prices, published in USD. Final currency, taxes, support allowances and any additional charges are confirmed before agreement. Existing agreements are governed by their terms.
